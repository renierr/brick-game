The goal

Drag to aim, release to fire a swarm of bouncing balls, and break every tile on the board before the tiles reach the bottom.

Each tile shows a number — its HP. Every ball that touches it takes 1 off. At zero the tile shatters and pays points. Clear the whole board and you advance a level, gain balls, and get a freshly generated layout.

But after every volley the entire board slides down one row. Let a tile cross the red danger line at the bottom and the run is over. There is no last level — only your best score.

The board, part by part

  1. Hidden rowsA tall layout can start above the top edge. Those rows slide into view one per turn, so new tiles keep arriving.
  2. TilesThe layout is cut from a hand-drawn stencil on a 13-column grid, so every board is a recognisable shape — a heart, an invader, a spider.
  3. Colour = toughnessOrange is soft, red and violet are tough. The number is the exact HP left.
  4. Ball pickupThe green (+) circle is not a tile and cannot be broken. Touch it with any ball and your stash grows by one, permanently.
  5. Danger lineThe dashed red line. If any tile ends a turn below it, the run ends.
  6. Charge chipsBanked pierce and bomb charges show here, always in the same spot, until your next volley spends them.
  7. LauncherWhere your balls come from, with the size of your stash on it. It moves to wherever the first ball came back down, so your next shot starts from there.
  8. Aim previewWhile you drag, the dashed line shows the path and the first tile you will hit — including bounces off the walls.

How one turn plays out

1 · AimDrag anywhere on the board. Straight sideways is not allowed — shots always go at least slightly upward.
2 · FireRelease, and your balls leave one after another along that line, up to 100 per turn. They bounce until every one drops off the bottom.
3 · DropThe whole board slides down one row and the launcher moves under your first returning ball. Then it is your turn again.
A volley that gets stuck can never trap you: hit Recall to bring the balls home at once, or Speed to fast-forward. If a volley runs long, the game speeds it up by itself.

When each tile appears

Special tiles reach the board three different ways, so no kind depends on a single layout being drawn:

  1. Baked into the stencil — hand-placed accents that are part of a shape: the invader's orb eyes, the castle's pierce towers. Only when that shape comes up.
  2. Rolled per tile — every plain tile independently rolls a small chance of being a bomb, a gift or a multiplier. These show up on nearly every board.
  3. Seeded per board — once the shape is laid out, each of the exotic kinds gets one chance to claim a few plain tiles. This is what keeps ramps and orbs in circulation. It is skipped when the shape already provides enough of that kind.

Unlock order

The exotic kinds hold back for the first levels, so you learn the basics before the board starts bending your shots. Before its unlock level a kind can still turn up, but only if its stencil happens to be drawn.

Where the layout can sit

  • A clear zone of about three rows above the danger line is always kept free, so no tile ever spawns inside your launch area and everything stays reachable.
  • At most four rows may start hidden above the top edge, and at least one row is always visible — a board is never entirely off-screen.
  • Shapes are centred on the grid and randomly mirrored, so the same stencil never feels quite the same twice.
  • HP keeps scaling with the level, and about one tile in eight is reinforced to roughly one and a half times the going rate.

Below the board

  • Recall (down arrow) — during a volley, pulls every ball home instantly and ends the turn. Nothing can ever leave you waiting on a stuck ball.
  • Speed (double chevron) — during a volley, each press stacks another boost: 3×, 6×, 9×, then 10×. It resets when the turn ends, and kicks in on its own if a volley drags past a few seconds.

Power-up menu

Open with the grid button. Every entry is unlimited — use them whenever you like.

Saving and starting over

  • Your level, score, ball count and the exact board are saved automatically. Close the tab mid-level and you pick up where you stopped.
  • A volley keeps resolving in real time even if you switch tabs mid-turn.
  • On game over, Retry Level puts the board back exactly as it looked when the level began. Start Over goes back to level 1.
  • Your best score survives everything.
